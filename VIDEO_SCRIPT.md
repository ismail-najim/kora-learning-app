# Kora — 3-Minute Video Script

**Total runtime:** 2:55 · **Format:** 1080p · **Music:** subtle ambient, fades on voiceover

---

## SCENE 1 · The Hook (0:00 – 0:18)

**VISUAL:** Quick montage — a student staring at a buffering video lecture, a Wikipedia wall-of-text, someone scrolling Khan Academy on patchy wifi.

**VO (calm, confident):**
> "Learning is a tool of hope. It makes people smart and innovative. It lifts people out of poverty. It's how we'll solve the hardest problems we face.
>
> But learning is broken. It's passive. It's gated by money. And if you don't have a connection today — you're left out.
>
> This is where Kora comes in."

**ON SCREEN (overlay):** *"Passive."* … *"Gated."* … *"Online-only."* → *"Until now."*

---

## SCENE 2 · Reveal (0:18 – 0:28)

**VISUAL:** Cut to the Kora home screen. Hero headline animates in: "Learn anything. Adapted to you."

**VO:**
> "Kora. An adaptive learning app powered entirely by Gemma 4. Running on this MacBook. Right now. With wifi off."

**ON SCREEN:** "✈️ Airplane mode" badge appears in the corner. Camera holds.

---

## SCENE 3 · Build the Profile (0:28 – 0:46)

**VISUAL:** Screen recording. User types **"How vaccines work"**. Hits Start Learning.

Loading screen pulses in — *"Drawing the threads together…"* + a brain-science insight card: *"Active recall beats re-reading by 3x."*

Cut to profile question: *"When something difficult finally clicked for you — what made it click?"* Four options with emoji.

User picks "📖 Someone told me a story."

**VO:**
> "Five questions. Kora figures out how *you* learn — story, steps, analogy, or doing. Visual or text. Fast or thorough. And it keeps adapting from there — not just what you say, but what you actually do."

Quick cut: questions 2, 3, 4, 5 fly past.

Reveal animation: "The Storyteller. I'll teach you through scenes, characters, and analogies."

---

## SCENE 4 · The Path (0:40 – 0:55)

**VISUAL:** Topic setup screen. User picks "📚 Solid · 45m" and "🤷 Nothing yet". Clicks Build My Learning Path.

Loading: *"Mapping the territory…"* with insight *"Forgetting is a feature — your brain weakens unused connections."*

**VO:**
> "Gemma 4 builds a path. Five modules. Each one sequenced to your level. Each one taught in *your* style."

---

## SCENE 5 · Hook → Teach (0:55 – 1:35)

**VISUAL:** Module 1 hook screen. Gradient headline: **"The Body's Memory of an Enemy"**. Hook text below. A big amber stat card: **"95%** — reduction in childhood deaths from measles after vaccination". A teal *"Counterintuitive"* card: "Vaccines don't kill viruses — they train your body to."

**VO:**
> "Every module opens with a hook. A scene. A stat. A paradox. The hook is the dopamine. It's what makes you want to continue."

User clicks. Loading insight: *"Mistakes wire learning."* Transition.

Teach screen renders. Camera pans down:
- Amber **Core Insight** card
- Four numbered sections with bold key terms
- **A rendered Mermaid SVG flowchart** showing how a vaccine triggers the immune response
- Amber **Worked Example** card
- Teal **Key Terms** grid (Antigen, Antibody, T-cell)
- Red **Common Confusion** callout

**VO:**
> "The teach screen has real diagrams — rendered SVG, not text. Key terms. Worked examples. And a callout for the misconception even smart students hold."

---

## SCENE 6 · Active Recall (1:35 – 2:00)

**VISUAL:** Activate screen. *"In your own words: how does a vaccine prepare your body to fight a future infection?"* User types into the box.

User types a partial answer: *"It teaches your immune system what the virus looks like."*

Click Submit. Loading. Feedback card appears:

> **Feedback · 65% there** — You named the core idea — pattern recognition by the immune system. Solid.
>
> **✨ What to add next time** — Mention **memory B-cells** — they're the long-term archive that makes the response 1000x faster the second time.

**VO:**
> "The grading is honest, not flattering. A vague answer is 30%, not 'good try, 70%'. And the feedback tells you the *one specific thing* to add — bolded, memorable, actionable. That's what your brain will keep."

---

## SCENE 7 · The Loop (2:00 – 2:25)

**VISUAL:** Quick cuts:
- The bridge screen connecting to the next module
- The session review screen with Gemma's adaptation message: *"You moved fast through text but slowed on the diagram. I'll add more visuals."*
- The home screen showing the user's session in "Continue learning" + a "Today's Reviews" badge

**VO:**
> "Concept. Learning. Test. Feedback. Next. That's the loop. After every session, Gemma rewrites your profile based on how you actually learned. Spaced repetition surfaces concepts the day before you'd forget them. You build, in your phone, a model of how *you* think."

---

## SCENE 8 · Why It Matters (2:25 – 2:45)

**VISUAL:** Globe shot. Pins drop on Mumbai, rural Kenya, Bristol. Each pin shows Kora running offline.

**VO:**
> "Eleven roles. One open-weight model. Zero cloud. Zero per-token cost. Zero login. Zero paywall around the next topic.
>
> A nurse in Kenya can upskill without wifi. A refugee student gets the same tutor as a Stanford freshman. A self-taught builder in Bristol learns by analogy, not by textbook. This is what on-device AI was for."

## Production Notes

**Tools:**
- Screen recording: macOS QuickTime (Cmd+Shift+5) at 1080p
- Editing: DaVinci Resolve (free) or CapCut
- Music: Epidemic Sound or YouTube Audio Library — search "ambient minimal" or "documentary calm"
- Voiceover: record on phone with the EarPods mic, or use ElevenLabs free tier with a calm male/female voice

**Shot list to capture before editing:**
1. Home screen (4s clean shot of hero)
2. Type "How vaccines work" → Start (full motion)
3. Profile question + click an option (full motion ×3)
4. Profile reveal (4s)
5. Topic setup → Build (full motion)
6. Loading screen with insight card (4s pause, let it read)
7. Hook screen — pan slowly down from headline to promise (8s)
8. Teach screen — pan slowly from top to bottom (12s)
9. Activate — type answer, submit, feedback (motion)
10. Bridge (4s)
11. Session review (8s)
12. Home with active sessions + reviews (4s)

**Tips:**
- **Don't speed-ramp** — let the user see what's on screen
- **Cursor highlight on** — makes recordings feel professional
- **Audio levels** — voiceover -3dB, music -18dB
- **Cold open** is most important; first 5 seconds decide if judges watch the rest
